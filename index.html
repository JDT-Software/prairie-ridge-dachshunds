Meet our dogs

The heart of Prairie Ridge.

Chloe and Kai are cherished members of our family and the foundation of our program.

“Chloe” means blooming

Chloe

In one word: gentle.

Chloe is the heart behind Prairie Ridge. From the moment she joined our family she has filled our home with love, joy, and countless precious memories. She is affectionate, calm, and happiest when she's surrounded by her people, whether she's relaxing on the couch, exploring outdoors, or (yes, really) enjoying bath time.

As our foundation female, Chloe represents everything we value: health, temperament, thoughtful breeding, and the special bond between dogs and their families. Watching her embrace motherhood has added a beautiful new chapter to her story.

Puppy process

From application to forever home.

Every puppy deserves the very best start. Our process is built around thoughtful breeding, loving care, early development, and finding the right families.

Start your application →
  1. 01

    Puppy application

    Tell us about your family, your lifestyle, and what you're looking for.

  2. 02

    Application review

    Each application is personally reviewed. Approval means you'll be considered for a puppy; it doesn't guarantee availability.

  3. 03

    Follow the journey

    We share pregnancy, birth, and puppy-development updates along the way.

  4. 04

    Puppies arrive

    Our focus turns to the health, care, and development of Chloe and her puppies.

  5. 05

    Puppy selection

    Approved families choose from available puppies, with our guidance on temperament, lifestyle, and preferences.

  6. 06

    Reservation

    Once a puppy is selected, we share next steps and reservation details privately.

  7. 07

    Preparing for home

    Puppies stay with mom until at least 8 weeks and get age-appropriate care before going home.

  8. 08

    Lifetime support

    Our relationship doesn't end at pickup. We're here for our Prairie Ridge families as their puppies grow.

The next chapter is growing

Every little milestone matters. As our Grace Litter grows, we're focused on thoughtful care, gentle handling, and meaningful experiences that lay the foundation for happy, confident companions, giving each little life the very best beginning we can.

Meet our family

Hi, we're Damian & Nadia.

We're the husband-and-wife team behind Prairie Ridge. Together with our two daughters and our beloved dachshunds, Chloe and Kai, we're a family that values faith, purpose, and the simple joys of everyday life.

Prairie Ridge began with our love for this wonderful breed and a desire to do things the right way, with patience, thoughtful planning, and a commitment to lifelong learning. What started as a love for Dachshunds has grown into something our whole family builds together.
